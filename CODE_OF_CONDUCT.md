# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want NoxaPlus Subtitle Studio to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of
experience level, language, nationality, gender identity, disability, appearance, religion,
or personal background.

We pledge to make participation harassment-free and to treat each other with respect.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Being kind and patient with questions, especially from first-time contributors.
- Giving specific, constructive feedback about code and behavior.
- Respecting different languages, writing systems, and accessibility needs.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, intimidation, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or comments.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Dismissing someone based on their language, accent, or writing style.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and code review, and in any public space where someone represents the
project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and other
contributions that do not follow this code. They may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently ban participants for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, or
harmful.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible. To report a problem, email
**NoxaPlus2025@gmail.com** with the subject "Code of Conduct". Include what happened, where,
and who was involved. Maintainers with a conflict of interest will not participate in the
decision.
